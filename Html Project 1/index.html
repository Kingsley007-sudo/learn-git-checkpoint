Sarissa King is a premium Greek spirit is a specialty that cannot be classified into a specific category of beverages. A unique spirit exclusively from Greece. The harmonious combination of aged wine distillates and Greek Muscat wine with the delicate and smooth taste of sun-dried grapes releases oak aromas and intense fruity elements, resulting in a rich, velvety taste and a delicate aroma.

The production

The distiller's expertise in selecting the finest Muscat wines and wine distillates and the masterful art of blending them in perfect proportions is the secret behind the recipe for this iconic drink. Rich, velvety taste, delicate aromas of caramel and vanilla. A warm and sweet symphony of flavors expressed with complexity.
